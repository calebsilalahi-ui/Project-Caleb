    02Problem

    Traditional education fails to teach digital wealth creation.

    Legacy schools and institutions teach theory. Young Indonesians are left with recycled PDFs and signal groups, and they pay for the difference with their own capital.

      Inner Circle

    07Marketing & sales

    The zero-CAC affiliate flywheel.

    Decentralized, performance-based marketing: affiliates earn 25% recurring, and only when a new subscriber pays.

    1. Live AMA is recorded
    2. AI turns it into short-form clips
    3. Affiliates post clips with their referral link
    4. Landing page, 3-minute VSL, checkout
    5. Bot onboards the member, who becomes an affiliate
